- **Part B** (A17 §1, §3.2; owner decisions 2026-10-24): 10 products, the two leaders set to the mean of the two largest of 10 N(0, 1) draws, the others redrawn below z_L − 0.5; scores equal z; K = 4; n = 1000; σ = 0.3; 2 equally likely states shifting the leaders by ±0.5. Policies: original (top K by u; match-quality-maximising in the deterministic limit), its separated version (Prop separation, ϖ from the deterministic limit), composition-blind (original or R†_L1, 1/2 each), and a quota implementation of the separated version. The H3a exact part and Θ_j use the exact σ → 0 limit; every other Part B metric is at σ = 0.3. 16 configurations (aligned / not aligned × baseline and 7 sensitivity levels), 200 markets each; costs on every product, quadratic and overflow, ω ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.6, 0.8, 1}, λ ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 2}.
- **H3c**: sponsorship on the not-aligned baseline markets (K^s = 1, 6 advertisers, θ ~ U[0, 1], δ = −0.5), M3 against M4 (quadratic cost λ = 0.5, ω = 0.5), with SL-19S's guardrail feasibility floor (owner decision 2026-10-24: the separated organic lottery is below 0.95 M^ref in most markets at σ = 0.3).
